A little help with your read

Direction: drag back from the ball, away from where you want it to go. Release to putt. Or use the aim buttons and Putt.

Pace: more power rolls farther. Uphill needs more pace; downhill needs less.

Break: dots move downhill. Aim uphill and let the slope curve your putt. Practice settings can show a predicted path or search for a makeable line.

Camera: drag away from the ball to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. C returns behind the ball; V gives a low view.

Keyboard: focus the green. Arrows aim and adjust power, Space putts, Escape cancels. R retries and N skips in practice. H opens settings.

Competition: fixed layouts, Stimp 10, grain on, stroke error off. No retries, skipping, or predicted/best lines. Scores are local to this browser.

This is a stylized simulation with exaggerated elevation and simplified cup behavior. Benchmark stats are illustrative, not validated coaching or PGA Tour measurements. No accounts, ads, or automatic tracking. Progress is stored in this browser; clearing site data removes it.
