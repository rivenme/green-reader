A little help with your read

Controls: choose Drag the ball or Aim buttons on the home screen or in Settings. Drag keeps the bottom panel hidden; pull back from the ball and release to putt. Aim buttons shows the slider and Putt button. Your choice is saved.

Stroke: the same pull produces the same strength between holes. Faster greens roll farther. Choose Short putts in Touch & cup for precision, or use Distance for an exact-feet assist. Adaptive distance restores the original sensitivity that changes with the lie.

Pace: the green bar grows with strength; its tip is not a predicted stopping point. A small meter appears beside the ball while dragging. Practice labels use a simulated roll without the cup: Good pace means finishing from 4 inches short to 18 inches past the cup line, with a controlled arrival. It is a game target, not a guarantee of a make. Starting line is assessed separately. A large sideways miss says Check starting line. No prediction means no claimed finish.

Break: dots move downhill. Aim uphill and let the slope curve your putt. In Practice, enable Predicted path & expected finish to see the simulated stopping point. With stroke error enabled, the preview is before random error.

Camera: the view stays still during your stroke and smoothly reframes the next putt once the ball stops. Turn off Reframe after each putt in Settings for manual framing. Drag away from the ball to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. C returns behind the ball; V gives a low view.

Practice: try the pace ladder, short-putt circle, and starting-line gate in Settings. After a miss, tap Details for coaching and a replay with the previous path. Soft/firm comparison shows two possible routes, not a prediction of your selected stroke.

Cup & fringe: forgiving cups accept every crossing; optional realistic cups let overly fast or shallow crossings roll over. Neither mode collides with the flag. The visible fringe slows the ball. At its outer edge, the ball stops for your next stroke without an extra penalty.

Keyboard: focus the green. Left/right aim; up/down adjust distance by 0.1 ft. Space putts, Escape cancels. R retries and N skips in practice. H opens settings.

Competition: fixed layouts, Stimp 10, grain on, stroke error off. No retries, skipping, or predicted/best lines. Scores are local to this browser.

This is a stylized simulation with exaggerated elevation and simplified cup behavior. Benchmark stats are illustrative, not validated coaching or PGA Tour measurements. No accounts, ads, or automatic tracking. Progress is stored in this browser; clearing site data removes it.
